You've got gorgeous jewellery. Your outfit is on point. But something still looks... off. Chances are, you're making one of these five incredibly common jewellery styling mistakes. Don't worry, they're easy to fix once you know what to look for.

## Mistake #1: Matching Everything Too Perfectly

Wearing a gold necklace with gold earrings, gold bangles, and a gold ring? That's not coordinated, that's a costume. Modern styling is about intentional mixing. Pair your gold necklace with rose gold rings, or mix metals entirely. The goal is to look curated, not uniform.

:::tip
The 70/30 rule: 70% of your jewellery in one metal tone, 30% in another. This creates visual interest while still looking cohesive.
:::

## Mistake #2: Ignoring Your Neckline

This is the #1 styling mistake. A choker with a high-neck top creates a cluttered look. A long pendant with a deep V-neck gets lost. Here's the rule: your necklace should follow the shape of your neckline, not fight against it.

- V-neck → pendant or lariat necklace
- Round neck → short chain or collar necklace
- Off-shoulder → choker or statement necklace
- High neck → skip the necklace, go bold on earrings

## Mistake #3: Wearing Statement Everything

Big earrings + big necklace + big bracelet = visual chaos. Choose ONE statement piece per outfit and let everything else support it. If you're wearing chandelier earrings, skip the necklace entirely or wear a thin chain. If you've got a bold necklace, stick to studs.

## Mistake #4: Forgetting About Proportion

Petite frames get overwhelmed by oversized pieces. Larger body types can carry bigger, bolder designs that might look out of place on a smaller frame. This isn't about rules, it's about what visually balances YOUR specific proportions.

## Mistake #5: Never Cleaning Your Jewellery

Even the most beautiful necklace looks dull when it's covered in a month of skincare residue, perfume buildup, and dust. A quick wipe with a soft cloth after every wear makes a dramatic difference. Your jewellery can only shine if you let it.

The best-dressed women aren't the ones with the most expensive jewellery, they're the ones who understand how to style what they have. Fix these five mistakes and you'll instantly look more polished, put-together, and confident.
